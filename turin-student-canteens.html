Guideline for Accessing University Canteens in Turin

ISOT Editorial Team • 2025-01-14 • ISOT Turin Guide

Welcome to our guide for navigating the university canteen services in Turin! This information will help you easily access affordable and convenient dining options throughout the city. Whether you’re looking for a quick bite or a full meal, here are the details you need to enjoy the meals at your university’s canteens.

1. Campus Piemonte ID Meal App (Required for Access)

To access the university canteens, students must use the Campus Piemonte ID Meal app, which ensures secure identification and prevents misuse. Here’s how to get started:

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Download the App

    • Search for “Campus Piemonte ID Meal” in the following stores:

      • Apple Store (iOS)

      • Google Play (Android)

      • AppGallery (Huawei)

 

  1. Associate Your Mobile Device

     

    • Visit the EDISU Piemonte website: www.edisu.piemonte.it

    • Log in to the Sportello Online using the temporary access code and password you received from your university.

    • Once logged in, click on Canteen App Association (“Associazione App Mensa”) and follow the instructions to generate a QR code. Scan the QR code with your mobile device to activate the app.

  1. Set Up a Personal PIN

    • Open the app and set a secure 5-digit PIN. This PIN is required for your transactions and should be kept confidential.

  2. Top Up Your Account

    • You can recharge your electronic wallet using either PagoPA or Virtual POS Unicredit. Choose your preferred method and follow the payment instructions in the app.

  3. Using the App at Canteens

    • When you are ready to have your meal, open the app and generate your dynamic QR code.

    • Show the QR code to the cashier at the canteen for quick and secure payment.

3. Operating Hours

Most canteens are open Monday to Friday, typically from 12:00 – 14:30 for lunch. Always check specific hours at each canteen before planning your visit.

4. Meal Options

Canteens typically offer two types of meals:

  • Full Meal (Pasto Intero): Includes a main course (primo), second course (secondo), a side (contorno), bread, water, and sometimes fruit or dessert.

  • Reduced Meal (Pasto Ridotto): A smaller portion that usually consists of a first course (primo), a side (contorno), bread, and water.

Some canteens offer special meals like pizza or insalata (salads) as alternatives, and specific locations may have their own menu variations.

5. Important Guidelines

  • Payment: Payments are made through the Campus Piemonte ID Meal app. The use of cash is typically not permitted.

  • Product Restrictions: Only products approved by the canteen will be served. No outside food or drinks are allowed inside the canteens.

  • Meals for International Students: If you are an international student, ensure you have your EDISU Piemonte card activated to get subsidized prices for meals.

6. Contact Information

For any issues with the Campus Piemonte ID Meal App, meal recharges, or canteen inquiries, please visit the EDISU Piemonte website or contact your university’s student services.

 

Enjoy your meals, and make sure to stay connected with the community by sharing your canteen experiences! Let us know if you need any further information or assistance.
