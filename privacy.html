Privacy Policy & Legal APS

Associazione di Promozione Sociale (ISOT APS) • Turin, Italy

1. Privacy Policy & GDPR Compliance

ISOT APS (International Students of Turin Associazione di Promozione Sociale) is committed to protecting the privacy of international students and community members in compliance with EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 2016/679 and Italian privacy laws.

Data Collected: When you complete our Free Community Access or Official APS Membership registration forms, we collect your name, email address, university affiliation, nationality, and city verification answer.

Purpose of Processing: Your personal data is used solely to issue membership receipts, send weekly community event invites, grant access to our official Telegram/WhatsApp groups, and manage non-profit association member registers. We never sell or share your data with commercial third-party advertisers.

2. Cookie Policy

The ISOT Community website uses strictly necessary session cookies and local storage tokens to provide seamless web navigation, hero photo gallery state, and form security verification.

No invasive third-party tracking cookies or advertising profile pixels are stored without your explicit consent. You can clear cookies at any time via your browser settings.

3. Statuto Associativo (ISOT APS Statute Summary)

Legal Form: ISOT is an officially registered non-profit Social Promotion Association (Associazione di Promozione Sociale - APS) operating under the Third Sector Reform (RUNTS) in Italy.

Non-Profit Principle: All association funds, membership fees (€10/year), and voluntary donations are strictly dedicated to fulfilling our institutional mission—fostering international student integration, hosting cultural festivals, and running career mentorship labs.

Member Rights: Official APS members have full rights to participate in general assemblies, inspect financial registers, vote for executive leadership, and receive formal payment receipts.
