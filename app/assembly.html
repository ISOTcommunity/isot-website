Assembly & Voting

How voting works

  • Who votes: ISOT APS members with a paid annual fee. One member, one vote.
  • Secret: ISOT records that you voted, never how you voted.
  • Final: once sent, a vote can't be changed.
  • Results: shown to everyone only after voting closes. Nobody sees them before, the Board included.

Board: set up a vote

Every member who can vote gets a notification. A vote can be edited or deleted only until the first ballot is cast.
