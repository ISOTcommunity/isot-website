Core Pillars & Programs

Building Bridges in Turin

Discover how ISOT supports every phase of your student journey—from cultural celebrations to housing help and career development.

Pillar 01

Cultural Events & Celebrations

Turin is home to students from all corners of the globe. At ISOT, we celebrate this multicultural rich tapestry through dedicated theme nights.

  • Nowruz (Iranian New Year): Annual celebration with authentic food, music, and traditional Haft-sin.
  • Turkish Culture Night: Turkish coffee, cultural exchange, and music evenings.
  • Brazilian Karaoke & Shamrock Parties: Lively social gatherings at Rough & Capodoglio.
View Weekly Schedule →
Pillar 02

Student Integration & Living Support

Moving to Italy requires navigating complex administrative steps. We provide clear, peer-tested guides and direct peer assistance.

  • Student Housing Guide: Finding apartments, avoiding scams, and lease contracts in Turin.
  • EDISU Scholarship Navigation: Payment deadlines, canteen access, and ranking details.
  • Permesso di Soggiorno & Healthcare: Step-by-step Post Italiane kit filing and medical registration.
Browse 13 Turin Guides →
Pillar 03

Growth & Career Lab

We help international students build competitive professional profiles, pitch startups, and connect with mentors in Turin.

  • SAS Startup Lab (Wednesdays @ SAA): 5-week incubator program taking student ideas from problem to live demo day pitch.
  • CV & Resume Clinics: Formatting Europass vs ATS resume standards for Italian and European recruiters.
  • Cofounder Matching & Personal Branding: Connecting student developers, designers, and business leads.
