How Your Contribution Helps

Student Welcome Kits

Covers printing SIM card guides, transit maps, and essential housing checklists for newly arrived students in Turin.

Cultural Festivals

Funds traditional food ingredients, music venues, and art supplies for Nowruz, Turkish Night, and multicultural meetups.

Emergency Relief

Provides small micro-grants for arriving students facing temporary accommodation crises or urgent healthcare registration fees.
