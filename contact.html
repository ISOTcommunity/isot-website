Frequently Asked Questions

Student FAQ Accordion

How do I join weekly ISOT events in Turin?
You don't need formal RSVP to attend our weekly meetups! Just show up at Monday Karaoke (Rough Bar @ 21:30), Thursday Social Hub (Capodoglio @ 21:00), or Saturday Pre-Party. All international students and youth are welcome.
What is the difference between Free Access & €10/yr Official Member?
Free Access grants entrance to all weekly social meetups and WhatsApp group chats. Official Membership (€10/year) grants official non-profit association status, voting rights at our General Assembly, priority access to partner programs like SAS Career Lab, partner discounts, and an official membership receipt.
How do I get help with student housing in Turin?
Read our comprehensive Student Housing in Turin Guide under the Turin Guide & Blog section. It outlines lease contract types, lease registration at Agenzia delle Entrate, and safe search platforms.
How does the EDISU scholarship payout work in Piedmont?
EDISU Piemonte releases scholarship payouts in installments (first installment typically around December). Make sure to upload your IBAN in your EDISU portal and check our dedicated EDISU Scholarship Guide on our Blog.
How can I register with the Italian National Health Service (SSN)?
Non-EU students can register for voluntary SSN health coverage by paying the annual F24 tax code slip at Post Italiane, then taking the receipt to their local ASL (Azienda Sanitaria Locale) office in Turin to select an English-speaking general practitioner.
Can I organize a cultural event or theme night with ISOT?
Yes! We love celebrating different cultures. Send us a message using the form above or message our team on Instagram @isot.community to propose a cultural theme night.
