The Problem

Every student starts
from zero.

🌍
You arrive in a new city and know nobody. The university gives you a student ID, not a social life.
📍
The city is full of things for you — courts, canteens, museums, venues — but nobody maps them for international students.
🤝
Student associations exist, but they are fragmented, informal and invisible online. There is no infrastructure.

Community is
infrastructure.

We did not start with a product. We started with a Thursday night. Then we built the infrastructure around what was already working.

The Differentiator

Compliance is
a feature.

Every Italian association carries the same legal machinery: a general assembly, motions, recorded votes, board elections, a volunteer register, consent records. Most run it on paper. ISOT runs it in the app.

Digital general assembly with quorum tracking and recorded attendance
Motions and member voting, with results stored and auditable
Board candidacies and elections run inside the platform
Volunteer register and membership validation, as RUNTS requires
Consent records and an activity log for data protection
