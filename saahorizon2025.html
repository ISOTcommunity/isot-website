ISOT Official Partner and Sponsor of SAA Horizon 2025

ISOT Editorial Team • 2025-03-19 • ISOT Turin Guide

ISOT Community is proud to announce our official partnership and sponsorship of the School Management of University of Turin (SAA) upcoming Career Development Event. This transformative event, scheduled for 3rd April 2025, is designed to empower students by bridging the gap between academic education and professional success. Through insightful networking opportunities, hands-on mentorship, and targeted skill-building sessions, participants will gain invaluable tools to navigate their careers with confidence.

A Multi-Stage Program for Career Excellence

This dynamic event will feature a structured, multi-stage program focused on professional development across key areas:

1. Freelancing, Management, and Startup Success
Students will have the opportunity to learn directly from accomplished professionals, including experienced freelancers, senior managers, and startup founders. This session will provide insights into building a successful freelance career, excelling in management roles, and launching a thriving startup. A Q&A session will follow, allowing attendees to engage with speakers and gain personalized advice.

2. Networking Mastery
Professional connections are essential to career growth. This segment will feature two networking experts who will share strategies for cultivating meaningful professional relationships. Following their insights, students will participate in a hands-on networking activity designed to build confidence and improve their networking skills in real time.

3. Interview Skills Workshop
Job interviews can be a crucial turning point in one’s career journey. In this session, two industry professionals will discuss effective interview techniques, strategies for making a lasting impression, and how to navigate challenging interview questions. The workshop will culminate in an interactive “speed-dating” style mock interview session, where students will receive direct feedback from HR professionals representing invited companies.

Why Attend?

By participating in this career development event, students will:

  • Gain firsthand knowledge from industry experts.

  • Develop essential skills in freelancing, management, and entrepreneurship.

  • Enhance their ability to network effectively in professional settings.

  • Refine their interview techniques through practical experience with HR professionals.

At ISOT Community, we are dedicated to fostering professional growth and innovation. Our collaboration with UNITO’s School of Management reflects our commitment to equipping students with the tools and resources necessary for success in today’s competitive job market.

Join us in 3rd April 2025 for this unparalleled opportunity to shape your future. Stay tuned for more details on event registration and the full speaker lineup!
