Weekly Event Hosts

Rough Bar

San Salvario

Located in the heart of San Salvario, Rough Bar is ISOT's official home for Monday Karaoke Night and Saturday Pre-Parties.

  • Exclusive drink discounts for ISOT members
  • Dedicated international stage & sound system
  • Address: Via Berthollet 11, Turin

Capodoglio

Murazzi del Po

Set right along the riverbank at Murazzi del Po, Capodoglio hosts ISOT's Thursday International Social Hub.

  • Riverfront outdoor terrace & indoor lounge
  • Weekly speed friending games & DJ sets
  • Address: Murazzi del Po 37, Turin
