ISOT (International Students of Turin) is the bridge between arriving in Italy and finding your true family in Turin.

Our Story & Mission

Moving to a new country to study at Politecnico di Torino, Università degli Studi di Torino, or SAA School of Management is thrilling—but navigating bureaucracy, housing, and social isolation can be daunting.

Founded by international students for international students, ISOT APS was built on a single core belief: No student should have to navigate their journey alone.

Today, ISOT is an officially registered Italian Social Promotion Association (APS), uniting young minds from over 40 countries through weekly cultural events, integration workshops, and professional career mentorship.

Core Principles

What Drives ISOT

Inclusion & Belonging

We foster a safe, warm environment where everyone—regardless of nationality, culture, or background—is welcomed as family.

Local Integration

We connect international youth directly with Turin's rich culture, university systems, local venues, and community initiatives.

Empowerment

Through our Career Lab clinics and leadership opportunities, we empower students to build valuable skills for their future careers.

APS Association Legal Notice

Associazione di Promozione Sociale (APS)

ISOT is formally registered under Italian non-profit law (RUNTS compliant APS). As an official APS entity, our leadership is democratically elected, our financial statements are open to members, and all revenues are directly reinvested into student support services.

President & Executive Board

Led by Amirhossein Shokouhipour and student board members overseeing operations, legal compliance, and community outreach.

General Assembly Rights

Official €10/yr APS members have full voting rights at our annual assembly and can run for board leadership positions.

Transparent Financials

All membership fees and donations are processed via our official Intesa Sanpaolo bank account with formal tax receipts issued.
